Title: Scaling Technical Communication: Expanding the LinkedIn Writer Voice Library

Sharing technical progress effectively requires more than just raw data; it requires the right perspective. To better support diverse engineering roles, we have significantly expanded the capabilities of the `linkedin-writer` skill.

Key updates:

- Integrated Voice Management: We moved `voice_styles.md` into the skill directory, improving portability and ensuring that style guidelines are always tightly coupled with the writing logic.
- Expanded Persona Library: We added five new specialized technical voices—The Architect, The SRE, The Product Engineer, The Security Researcher, and The Data Scientist—bringing the total to ten distinct personas.
- Improved Accessibility: All new voices are immediately available for use via the `--voice` parameter, allowing for seamless switching between different communication goals and audiences.

By providing these tailored personas, we are making it easier for engineers to communicate their work with authenticity and strategic impact, whether they are discussing systems architecture, operational reliability, or user-centric product development.
