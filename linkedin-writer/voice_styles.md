# Voice Styles

These new voices are now available for use with the `--voice` parameter in the `linkedin-writer` skill.

## Professional
- **Tone**: Professional, analytical, and authoritative.
- **Goal**: Communicate technical progress and strategic value to a broad professional audience.
- **Constraints**: Use industry-standard terms, maintain high signal-to-noise ratio, and strictly no emojis.

## Hacker
- **Tone**: Raw, technical, and fast-paced
- **Goal**: Share technical learnings with other developers
- **Constraints**: Use technical jargon, keep it brief, no fluff

## Minimalist
- **Tone**: Extremely concise, impactful, and direct.
- **Goal**: Deliver the core message with zero overhead.
- **Constraints**: No more than 3 bullet points, maximum 2 sentences per point.

## Mentor
- **Tone**: Encouraging, educational, and humble.
- **Goal**: Help others learn from recent mistakes or successes.
- **Constraints**: Must include a "Lesson Learned" section, use accessible language.

## Visionary
- **Tone**: High-level, inspiring, and future-oriented.
- **Goal**: Connect current technical tasks to broader industry shifts.
- **Constraints**: Focus on the "why" and "what's next" rather than the "how".

## The Architect
- **Tone**: Structured, trade-off oriented, and systemic.
- **Goal**: Explain the structural decisions and long-term implications of technical choices.
- **Constraints**: Must mention at least one trade-off (e.g., latency vs. consistency), focus on scalability.

## The SRE
- **Tone**: Pragmatic, data-driven, and slightly cynical.
- **Goal**: Share insights on system reliability, incident response, and operational excellence.
- **Constraints**: Mention metrics (SLAs/SLOs), focus on "what happens when things fail".

## The Product Engineer
- **Tone**: Empathetic, iterative, and value-focused.
- **Goal**: Bridge the gap between technical implementation and user impact.
- **Constraints**: Focus on "the user experience", mention feedback loops or rapid prototyping.

## The Security Researcher
- **Tone**: Paranoid, meticulous, and adversarial.
- **Goal**: Highlight potential risks, privacy concerns, and the importance of robust design.
- **Constraints**: Use terms like "threat model", "attack surface", or "least privilege".

## The Data Scientist
- **Tone**: Evidence-based, nuanced, and experimental.
- **Goal**: Communicate findings from data, the results of experiments, and the "why" behind model choices.
- **Constraints**: Reference statistical significance, data quality, or model bias.
